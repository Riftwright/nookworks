# Hand-finished textures

Most of the mod's custom textures are generated by the `gen_*.py` scripts beside this file. The PNGs listed
here are the exception: they were drawn and finished by hand, and the files in
`src/main/resources/assets/nookworks/textures/block/` are the master copies. Nothing regenerates them.

No script may write these paths. If a generator ever needs to touch one of them, remove it from this list
first — otherwise a routine `python tools/textures/gen_*.py` would quietly overwrite artwork that cannot be
recovered from code.

`block/retort_front.png` and `block/retort_side.png` are the exception to the rule above: they are built
from the lit ones by `gen_retort_cold.py`, which puts the fire out and copies every other pixel across, so
the cold faces are still the same hand-finished art. `block/spawner_controller_screen_off.png`, the console's
screen while it is not running, is built the same way from `spawner_controller_screen.png` by
`gen_spawner_screen_off.py`: the glass inside the bezel goes dark, every other pixel copied across. The Spawner Link
Sender's and Receiver's `*_off.png` (their lines dark, at rest) and `*_glow.png` (the lines alone, laid glowing over
the working block) are built from their faces by `gen_spawner_links.py`, the steel copied across untouched.
`block/experience_tank_front.png`, the face the tap is on, is built from `experience_tank_side.png` by
`gen_experience_tank_front.py`: the window closed over in the panel's own steel, every other pixel copied across.
`block/magnet_chest_side.png` is built the same way from
`magnet_chest_front.png` by `gen_magnet_chest_side.py`: boards where the front has its plate, every other pixel
copied across.

The Generator's, Retort's, Mercury Tank's and Experience Tank's models (`tools/gen_machine_models.py`) set each
panel 1 px back inside the frame drawn round its edge; they lay these faces exactly where a cube would, so no pixel
of them moves.

All of them are 16x16 and fully opaque: the blocks that used to be a vanilla texture with a cutout overlay
laid over it are now plain cubes (or, for the Spawner Controller, a model of its own). (One pixel of
`block/spawner_controller_console.png`, at 3,6, was at alpha 254, which made the whole sprite count as
translucent; it was set to 255 with the author's leave on 2026-10-03, its colour untouched.)
