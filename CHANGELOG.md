# Changelog

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-06)
- 1.4.0: machines easier to work - a click opens a machine's screen whatever you hold, shift-clicks go as on a furnace, buckets fill the Sealed Retort's and Generators' tanks, a click on a machine's arrow opens its JEI recipes, the Iron Pot says why it cooks nothing - you wade and swim through oil, biodiesel, liquid experience and mercury, the tableware is redrawn round, and add-ons (nookTechnology, the industry add-on) can add pages to the Spawner Controller and are warned of on the parts screen. A 1.3 client cannot join a 1.4 server. Worlds from every earlier version keep everything in them.
- Fixed: shift-clicking into an add-on's page on the Spawner Controller that holds nothing for now (nookTechnology's Mob Data Bank without power) could freeze the server.
- Fixed: the Recycler shredded machines still holding mercury, water or fuel; they are turned down until emptied, as a filled chest is.
- Fixed: an Iron Pot set on a Grill had no owner, so a claimed pipe line could fill it; the Stirring Tank pushed biodiesel across a claim's edge; placed bottles could be taken and Drive Wheel belts taken off in adventure mode.
- Fixed: the Barter Terminal turned a named (or an add-on's) Mob Bottle plain when it took a piglin out; an enderman brewed into a spawn egg lost the block it carried.
- Changed: a water bucket clicked on the Sealed Retort fills its water tank, and a bottle or bucket of vegetable oil or biodiesel fills a Generator's fuel tank; a mercury bucket held to a Copper Tank is turned down instead of poured out beside it.
- Changed: the Rice Polisher and Oil Press give back what was put in when nothing is made yet (sneak with an empty hand), as the Hand Quern does; a hand machine says why it turns something down; putting things in and taking them out of the hand machines makes a sound.
- Fixed: a hammer's 3x3, tree felling and vein mining went off again for every block another mod breaks for the player (FTB Ultimine); blocks a hammer, felling or vein mining takes now crumble and sound for the one breaking them too (the nearest 16).
- Fixed: Generators gave no power to other mods' cables that keep none themselves; a Hub Stone pushed by a piston left lamps beyond it lit.
- Fixed: a Printing Press tab clicked mid-print stopped the print; the Camera clicked and flashed on servers with photos off; Recipe Choice could forget a pick some time after a death; a return door with no home could send a player into an If waiting for its reset; a die in a turned structure lay at its old pose; an empty main hand took a hung lantern or vine down instead of letting the off hand's flint and steel or bone meal act; flint and steel at an If portal went on to the off-hand item.
- Changed: machine screens: a click on a machine's arrow (or the Generator's flame) opens its JEI recipes; hover texts break at a steady width (Japanese and Chinese between words); "Tank full" is amber and says how to draw the tank off; the redstone button stands in one place on every screen; the Laser's beam shows its numbers along its whole length; a shift-click shows the item landing where the server puts it.
- Changed: the Generator, Diesel Generator, Electric Furnace, Electrolytic Cell, Sealed Retort, Barter Terminal and Creative Generator show their front in item frames, on shelves and in hand; the Mug, Wine Glass, Plate, Dice, Bookend, stools, Iron Pot and Glazed Flower Pot are drawn bigger in the inventory, and the Energy Cable a size up; support beams' and brackets' grain is no longer stretched; a broken Warp Anchor crumbles into pieces of its ring.
- Changed: the Magnet Chest opens and closes with a sound; sifting by hand makes its sound once, not twice.
- Changed: wooden chairs, stools, benches and desks burn as fuel.
- Changed: text: "right-click" where three pages said "click" (Ventilation Fan, the Experience Tank's tap, Hand Quern); in Spanish and Portuguese the parts screen tells spawners from Generators; Fine Shears are 上質なハサミ; small Russian and Chinese term fixes; an empty Mob Bottle an add-on makes take any health says so.
- Added (for add-ons): `MobBottleItem.anyHealthWhen`, `StorageSpill.takePending`, `Features.addOnParts`, `#nookworks:spawner_pickup_tools`; the Mob Bottle now hears a click on a mob at the lowest priority (docs/compat.md).
- Changed: a click on a machine with a screen opens it whatever you hold - nothing goes in by a click any more, so a full hotbar no longer means emptying a hand, and planks or ore are never swallowed by a Generator or Electric Furnace by mistake. Fill machines in their screen, or by hopper and pipe; buckets and bottles still pour by a click. The hand machines (Hand Quern, Noodle Maker, Rice Polisher, Oil Press) and sifting by hand are as before.
- Changed: the Valve, the Ventilation Fan and the Experience Tank's tap switch at a click with anything in hand, as a lever does; sneak to set a pipe or block against them.
- Fixed: the Wrench could not turn an empty Valve or Sight Glass without sneaking.
- Changed: a click that is turned down always says why: rope that can go no lower, a bookend row with no room, a Nested Timer where you may not build.
- Added (for add-ons): a public way to bottle a mob at any health, the filled bottle keeping the empty one's components, and to break blocks as extra ones that vein mining and tree felling leave alone (nookTechnology's Reinforced Mob Bottle and Heavy Hammer); Earthmover goes on a tag of its own, #nookworks:earthmover_tools, that other mods' tools can join.
- Changed: the tableware and dishes are redrawn round: the Plate has a raised rim, udon comes in a round bowl with a fishcake and chopsticks, tonkatsu with a heap of cabbage and mustard, karaage on a lettuce leaf with a lemon wedge, the donut is a round ring, and the Mug has a lip, a dark inside and a proper handle.
- Fixed: stepping into Vegetable Oil, Biodiesel, Liquid Experience or Mercury left you (and any mob) stuck fast in it, not even sinking. You wade and swim through them as through water now: oil a little thicker, mercury as thick as lava and too heavy to swim up through (you climb out at its edge, as out of lava).
- Changed: other mods' things fit in more places: the Fried Egg counts as a cooked egg and the Onion as an onion for other mods (common tags), and the Rice Bale takes any mod's rice. Blocks of ours that remember other places (Drive Wheels, Item Warps, Spawner Links and Controller, the Dock-a-Daemon Door) are marked so Create's contraptions do not move them.
- Changed: a campfire item tells you it is placed unlit while that setting is on, and a group's box on the parts screen shows a dash when only some of its parts are on.
- Fixed: Mercury's JEI page said a bucket could not go into a machine; since 1.3 it pours in at the machine's gauge.
- Added (for add-ons): other mods can add pages to the Spawner Controller's screen, with slots of their own and the player's inventory; the controller then shows tabs, its settings first. Without such an add-on it is as before.
- Added: with an add-on built on Nookworks installed (nookTechnology), switching off a part it needs warns first: the parts list counts that mod's recipes the change would stop (with the Machine Chassis off, 39 of nookTechnology 0.2.0's), and it can still be switched off. A world whose parts already stop some says so to an operator or the world's owner on joining, and in the server log.
- Fixed: an Iron Pot over the fire that cooked nothing said nothing; it now says how much water or oil the dish needs, or that nothing cooks from what is in it yet. Under a bottle of water or oil left in a pot could not be got out: an empty bottle or bucket tips it out now, when nothing is cooking. A dish served into the last bowl in hand (or taken by hand) goes off the hand, so the next click does not put a Bowl of Rice back in.
- Changed: a Mercury Bucket clicked on the Stirring Tank or Electrolytic Cell pours into it, as at its gauge; the Sealed Retort and Vacuum Distiller, which make mercury, turn it down at their gauge too.
- Changed: a 1.3 client can no longer join a 1.4 server (or the reverse): it is told at login, instead of being dropped at its first click on an Iron Pot.
- Fixed: a Stirring Tank fed oil or water from above pushed its biodiesel up into that feed, stopping the line; the top only sends biodiesel out when nothing feeds it from there.
- Fixed: a Filament Bulb with no room under a Thin Rope, and a thing hung too near a post or another on a span, were turned down without a word; berries taken off a Hook were lost with block drops off; a door, trapdoor or fence gate a wind charge swung open lost its oil and coating; a placed log burnt away left a mark that a tree later grown there would not fell through.
- Fixed: the Recycler dropped what it made into lava, fire or water beside it; a Lode Pearl set its thrower down beyond the world border (it says so now, and comes back still bound); a return door led into an If waiting for its reset; a Spawner Link Sender or Trading Counter set down by another mod's machine could be used by no one; the Camera's own messages were hidden by its viewfinder; the Nested Timer's sliders jumped back after a drag and lost quick arrow-key steps.
- Fixed: with the Sifter, Sealed Retort or Machine Chassis switched off but the kitchen on, bread had no recipe at all (its dough needs salt); vanilla's three-wheat bread is back while the dough cannot be made.
- Fixed: nookTechnology's red-hot steel gave Nookworks' hidden velarium advancement "Too Hot to Handle"; a Creaking was told to be brought to half health for a Mob Bottle, which no hit can do (it does not go in one now).
- Fixed: raising energyUse or generatorOutput in play took full effect only after the chunk reloaded; saving This World's Parts in a world with no parts file wrote the parts the start-up config switches off as left out by the world.
- Changed: mercury poured into a machine or at a gauge sounds as it does at a Mercury Tank; a hand machine turned several times a tick by a geared Motor plays its sound once.
- Changed: texts: the Generator's and Sealed Retort's tanks take buckets and bottles, a set-down Filament Bulb switches at a click whatever is in hand; Korean red-hot tooltip, Russian Nether Brick and book names, the Spanish, Portuguese and Russian glass bottle, Portuguese Sort button, Traditional Chinese lever, Japanese screen paths, settings name and vanilla names (試練の間, ねじれツタ, コンブ, ラピスラズリ).
- Changed: while campfires are set down unlit (the server setting Campfires Placed Unlit, on by default), a campfire or soul campfire item is an unlit one in the hand too: its icon is the unlit campfire, and under a shader pack it no longer lights up the ground round you, only to go dark as you set it down. One copied lit in creative still looks and shines lit. With the setting off, campfires are entirely vanilla's again: placed lit, with the burning icon and the light in the hand.
- Changed: machine screens shift-click as the furnace's does: what the machine does not take moves between the inventory and the hotbar (it used to stay put), and a result comes out to the hotbar first, an input to the inventory first. The same on the Spawn Egg Brewer, Printing Press and Repair Box.
- Changed: the Block of Velarium is redrawn as cut crystal facets in soft gradients, with no rim, so a wall of it shows no grid.
- Changed: text: the Motor's page gives its speed as turns - two a second, a quarter round each as a hand's click, so its shaft goes round once every two seconds (30 rpm); in Spanish, Portuguese and Russian the Vacuum Distiller and Sealed Retort are now the Destilador / Дистиллятор and Retorta / Реторта, short enough for their screens' titles.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-01)
- 1.3.0: dishes set out on Plates and Wooden Trays, tableware, the Standing Mirror, the Warp Anchor, chimneys in more stone with Flues, pouring at the tanks' gauges, sorting turned round, the sorters' row turned round, the Trading Counter waiting out high prices - and every part of the mod can be switched off, with what goes along shown first. Worlds from every earlier version keep everything in them.
- Fixed: models that showed a gap or the ground through them from some angles - the ends of valves and sight glasses and of pipe collars, the undersides of the workbenches' aprons and drawer, the oil lamp's wick, the Spawn Egg Brewer's stopper and foot, the Trading Counter's foot rail, placed books, the Absorption Pad's riser, the ends of desk rails at a corner, and the wall shelf in the hand.
- Added: on a machine's screen, click a tank's gauge with a bucket or bottle on the cursor to pour it into that tank, or with an empty one to fill it from the tank - water into an Electrolytic Cell by the bottle, mercury into a mercury tank by the bucket, water back out of a Stirring Tank. Only what the gauge holds goes in or out.
- Changed: the Oil Press, Hand Quern, Noodle Maker and Rice Polisher send on what they make as a dropper does: into a hopper or chest under them, as before, else one in front, else out of the spout or chute at their front, dropping onto the ground just there - where a hopper set in the floor or a Magnet Chest gathers it. So an Oil Press can have a Copper Pipe under it for its oil and still have its cakes gathered, and a machine left with nothing round it no longer fills up and stops. With a block in front (a carpet is no wall), what it makes waits inside as before.
- Added: the Trading Counter's screen has "Stop when prices rise": ticked, a trade whose price the villager's demand has raised waits until it is back at its own price (your discount still counts), then trades again; unticked, it trades at whatever the price is, as before. Its status line says when it waits for prices.
- Added: a click on the Keep Out row's sign, on both sorters, turns the row round: what is in it is all that is drawn in from the container above (and all a hopper can put in), everything else staying there. The sign turns into a green tick and the row's frames green; another click puts it back.
- Added: on chests and Storage Barrels, a button between the sort order's and Sort turns the order round - ascending or descending: fewest first, the game's order from its end, the mods Z-A, first added first. Choosing another order puts it back to its own way; a barrel keeps it with its order, a chest's is your own setting.
- Added: an item tag Pressable (#nookworks:pressable) of what the Oil Press takes, so a Precision Sorter can send seeds off to a press. A datapack's new pressing entries need adding to it as well.
- Changed: a Copper Pipe joined to the side of an Oil Press meets a copper port on its tray's rim, instead of ending in the air beside its thin frame. Pipes work as before.
- Changed: a pipe joined to a machine whose panels are set back - the Generator, Diesel Generator, Electric Furnace, Electrolytic Cell, Sealed Retort, Stirring Tank, Distiller, Water Pump and the Copper, Mercury and Experience Tanks - meets an iron flange on the panel, instead of its end standing a pixel off the machine in the air.
- Changed: an End Seed can also open into other mods' flowers, with the same chance as vanilla's. The server settings End Seeds Grow Mods' Flowers and Mods Left Out of End Seeds turn this off, or leave out a mod.
- Changed: a Motor with more than eight machines on its belts counts only those with work among its eight: a machine with nothing to do no longer takes the place of one that has something. Lines of eight or fewer turn as before.
- Added: the Plate (white terracotta), to serve one dish on, and tableware to set the table with - the Wine Glass and the Mug, up to four to a block. Udon, tonkatsu, karaage and donuts on a Plate or a Wooden Tray are set out as the dishes themselves: udon in its bowl, the cutlet sliced with cabbage, karaage with lemon, a glazed donut.
- Added: Chimneys in stone brick, nether brick and cobblestone beside the brick one, each with its clay pot, and a Flue of each kind: a whole block of the chimney's masonry with the flue down its middle, for where a chimney goes through a roof or a floor. Smoke a Range Hood draws into its Ducts goes up through Flues and out of the chimney's pot; a Flue is open only at its top and bottom, so Ducts join it there and not on its sides.
- Added: the Warp Anchor (Dark Matter, obsidian and amethyst shards): a floating cube in ender pearl colours inside a turning ring. While on, nothing warps into or out of the space around it (4 blocks each way across, 2 up and down - a spawner's spawning area and a block more): endermen and shulkers cannot teleport there, and ender pearls, Lode Pearls and chorus fruit do not take you in or out. Endermen stay out of a spawner room, and a shulker spawner can be made into a farm. Elevators, the Dock-a-Daemon Door and Item Warps are not stopped. Right-click to turn it on and off.
- Changed: the Nookworks parts list (Create World > More) has a checkbox for each part and All On / All Off buttons, in groups (Industry, Storage and Logistics, Tools, Kitchen and Home, Wonders), each with a box that switches all of it. It can also be opened in a world, from Mods > Nookworks > Config > This World's Parts: a change takes effect the next time the world is opened; on a server only an operator can change it.
- Added: every part of the mod can be switched off now, the machines everything else is made through too - the Machine Chassis, Sifter, Crusher, Sealed Retort, Stirring Tank, Vacuum Distiller and Oil Press. Whatever nothing can make any more with a part off goes with it: with the Electrolytic Cell off there is no caustic soda, so no paper, posters, labels, Printing Press, soap or biodiesel either. Before saving such a change, the parts list shows everything it takes with it, by name, to switch off anyway or go back; a part left with nothing that can be made is shown off. Packs whose other mods make such things another way can keep them with the start-up setting Items Kept.
- Fixed: with Velarium switched off, the Velarium Hammer's and the Velarium Storage Barrel's recipes were still there, though the items were not.
- Fixed: a machine with no chest under it and blocks both under it and on top (a Crusher on stone with stone over it, a Vacuum Distiller under its column) set what it made down inside the block on top, which pushed it out wherever it could go; it now drops it beside itself, and with every side closed keeps it until there is room.
- Fixed: a Storage Barrel too full to break counted as someone else's land to a neighbour's machines: a sorter, warp or magnet chest of another player could not take from it or put into it.
- Added: the Standing Mirror, a tall mirror in a Dawnwood frame that reflects what stands before it - the room, you, and whoever is about - from wherever you stand. The If Mirror's recipe without the Dice. The two nearest in view show their reflections, with Sodium and Iris shader packs too.

## 1.2.1 (2026-10-26)
- 1.2.1: a powered Motor runs until a redstone signal stops it, the Oil Can quiets it, the Magnet draws experience fast and takes it at once, and felled trees' leaves for those who asked: a Silk Touch axe drops them as leaves, and a setting takes them all at once. Worlds from every earlier version keep everything in them.
- Added: a hidden advancement for taking a Red-Hot Velarium Ingot in a bare hand (a viewer's idea).
- Changed: a powered Motor runs - with work or without, with a machine on its shaft or none - until a redstone signal stops it, as a motor would (it stood still until a machine it turned had work, which puzzled anyone used to Create). Idling it draws 2 FE/t; turning machines, 20 FE/t for each at work, as before. Motors already set down start turning, and their Generators burn a little more for it. Its screen says Idling, or Stopped while a signal holds it.
- Added: the Oil Can on a Motor silences its hum, as it does a door's or a piston's.
- Changed: a Magnet draws experience orbs twice as fast, and takes the orbs that reach you all at once instead of one every other tick, as vanilla does; within vanilla's own reach of 8 blocks the orbs drifted in anyway and the Magnet made little difference.
- Added: a tree felled by an axe with Silk Touch drops its leaves as leaves, as that axe breaks them, instead of saplings, sticks and apples (a player's request). It wears the axe a point for every four leaves - a quarter of what cutting them costs - and stops before the axe would break, with Protect Tools on. Sneak and right-click in the air with the axe to switch this off (and on again) for that axe; its tooltip says which.
- Added: the config option Instant Leaf Decay (off by default): with Fast Leaf Decay on, a felled tree's leaves all go at once, the moment it comes down, instead of over a second or two (a player's request).

Worlds from every earlier version keep everything in them.

- Fixed: an Iron Pot's line on the action bar (an empty hand's click) ran off the screen with many kinds in it - cream stew's six did at 1280x720; it is fitted to the screen it is shown on: shorter (no count of one, the pot's room left out), then the first things and how many kinds more.

- Fixed: the Compressor's slot took another mod's dust or gem with no 9-to-1 recipe (or brown rice with the kitchen off) on a shift-click and threw it straight back; the server tells each player exactly what presses there.
- Fixed: a machine's progress could read past 100 % for a tick after the machine-time setting was lowered, on its screen and in Jade.
- Fixed: mobs took a Corrugated Sheet lying in the middle of its block (the Wrench) for open air, and stuck under one at head height; it is a slab's floor to them now.
- Fixed: a belt of Rope or Thin Chain named at an anvil came back unnamed when taken off or snapped; it comes back as it was.
- Fixed: on a server with claims, a stranger's belt tied before a claim was made over its line kept the claim's owner from building there; it snaps now as they build, its rope dropped, as a block a piston pushes in snaps it.
- Fixed: on a server with claims, a stranger's Recycler whose intake alone touched a claimed Energy Cable ran on the claim's power (its body touching it was refused); and a stranger's machines beyond a claimed Motor's wheels, which it may not turn, took up its eight and left the owner's own still.

- Changed: the Compressor's page in JEI says its screen takes things by hand too.
- Added: more parts a pack can switch off (nookworks-startup.toml, and on the Create World screen): the Generators (machines then run on another mod's power), the Electric Furnace, the Planter, the Water Pump, the Motor, the Electrolytic Cell, the Stone Maker, and Velarium (its ingots, tools and armour, with the Velarium Hammer, Sorter Gear, Storage Barrel and Upgrade). Each was checked to leave everything else still to be had. The advancements that hung from the Generator and the Electrolytic Cell hang a step higher, so a pack switching those off keeps the rest.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-20)
- 1.1: books to lay down and a Bookend to stand them in, recycling (the Recycler, the Compressor and building with scrap), lanterns and glow berries on Thin Rope and Thin Chain with a lever at the post, egg dishes and campfires set down unlit, and Spanish, Russian and Brazilian Portuguese. Worlds from every earlier version keep everything in them.
- Added: books lie on a block - sneak and right-click the top of one with a book, an enchanted book, a written book or a book and quill; up to three in a pile, each turned its own way but their middles over one another, so a pile looks put down in passing yet stands; its cover shows what it is (an enchanted one gives off the enchanting table's glyphs now and then). Used on a wall's side instead, books stand leant against the wall, cover out, up to three one in front of another. Each keeps itself, enchantments and pages; an empty hand takes the top one back, and broken the pile drops them.
- Added: the Bookend, a thin L of iron (three iron nuggets make two). Set on a block's top, books used on it stand upright against it, spines out, five to a block and on into the next while there is floor, as long a row as you like - on a desk, a shelf or the middle of the floor, where no wall is; take the bookend away and the row falls. ## 0.1.0 — first release

- Vein mining: break an ore with a suitable pickaxe to mine the connected vein (sneak for one block).

- Elevator (smooth stone, 8 smooth stone + ender pearl): jump to go to the next elevator above, sneak for the one below (same X/Z, needs 2 blocks headroom, range 64).
- Item Warp sender/receiver: items dropped on or hoppered into the sender go to the nearest receiver in the column (into the container below it, or dropped).

- Sifter: sift gravel, sand, cobblestone, deepslate, netherrack, soul sand into random resources, by hand or hopper-fed; datapack-extensible.
- Fireproof Coating: coated flammable blocks never burn (fire, spread, lava); per-position, cleared when broken; sparkle hint while held.
- Item Sorter (iron/gold/diamond/netherite): ghost-filter GUI, registered items right / others left, swappable, fills inventories or drops.
- Storage Barrels (iron/gold/diamond/netherite): 27/54/81/108 slots holding 2/4/16/256 stacks each (iron is a barrel and 8 iron; netherite takes a netherite block at the smithing table); upgrading keeps contents. They are barrels of faded staves banded in their tier's metal, with a square lid that opens the whole top, and face any way as a barrel does.

- Redstone Cogstone: a stone block that carries redstone in all six directions (one less per block) and powers its neighbours.
- Magnet (range 8) and Advanced Magnet (range 16): right-click to toggle; pulls items and XP; sneak to pause.
- Magnet Chest: a 27-slot chest that vacuums up dropped items within 5 blocks.
- Mob Bottle (catch a mob at half health or less, release it again) and Spawn Egg Brewer (bottle + egg + blaze powder = spawn egg).
- Mercury processing: Sealed Retort (cinnabar to mercury + sulfur dust), Stirring Tank (gold, other metal ores or rock + mercury to amalgam), Vacuum Distiller tower (amalgam to metal, mercury recovered); iron Mercury Tank and Pipe (mercury stays out of other mods' fluid systems); toxic liquid mercury.

- Chlor-alkali chain: the Sealed Retort boils a bucket of sea water down to Crude Salt (bucket returned), the Sifter washes it into Salt, and the Stirring Tank plus Vacuum Distiller run it as a mercury cell into Caustic Soda, recovering 95% of the mercury.
